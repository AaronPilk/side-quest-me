## Cross-source pattern on what makes a good side quest
Every source that explains itself lands on the same five things: it's optional and low-stakes; it's novel (a place, food, route or skill you haven't tried); it's finishable in one sitting, often under an hour; it has a tiny concrete "win" (a photo, a ranking, a stranger's answer, a thing made); and the sillier or more specific the framing, the better it lands. Talking to strangers and going somewhere you've never had a reason to go recur across almost all of them. Two sources (Coffee with Steph, Pete Cataldo) already gamify with points/XP tiers, and two (One More Sidequest, Pursuit of It All) tier by time budget — both are natural axes for the app.
