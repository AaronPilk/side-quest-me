# Internal iPhone beta — 1.0.0 (4)

This beta connects to the live Sidequest account, quest and video services. Videos and new Series stay private until you explicitly publish them.

Please try:

- Focus on **Create → Draft with AI** for this build's changes. Try the same
  theme with different groups and intensities. Confirm your saved interests,
  participation preferences and boundaries influence the proposal while the
  current outing's exact group, budget and time remain authoritative. Full Send
  now has stricter audience/experience checks. It may return a clear no-fit
  message; please report those cases with the plan and brief you entered.
- In the AI plan, choose a venue and try the age eligibility, venue permission
  and optional adult-nightlife choices. These are self-declarations, not age
  verification. Change the group, headcount or setting and check that old
  confirmations and venue costs are cleared instead of carried to a new plan.
- Quest quality is still being tuned. A live five-friends Full Send evaluation
  returned ideas that were too mild and were rejected. This build does not yet
  connect nearby Apple Maps places or live events to AI generation. The ordinary
  Create recommendations still use the existing catalog.
- On a fresh signed-in account, follow the new setup: personal or brand,
  optional nickname, optional ChatGPT summary, then one preference question at a
  time with progress. Skip a question or choose **Finish later → Save and explore
  for now**. Unknown answers should stay unknown, saved account details should
  persist, and incomplete preferences should remain easy to resume from Profile.
- Open **Profile → Account & quest preferences** or the matching Settings entry.
  Edit a few answers, return and refresh. Saved answers should survive; explicit
  Reset should stay unknown. Try the direct Account details and ChatGPT summary
  shortcuts, including immediate summary removal and save-error recovery.
- Tap **Copy prompt and open ChatGPT** on a physical iPhone with ChatGPT
  installed, then with the app absent to exercise the external browser fallback.
  Paste/send the prompt yourself, return to Sidequest and paste a reviewed
  summary. Background and return during the journey; the current question and
  pending summary should resume. Copy/launch failures should provide manual
  recovery. Text alone should never create confirmed preferences.
- Accept a quest, record or import one video, then use **Turn into a series** on the quest or in your journal. The screen starts with your quest's title and premise. Your saved video and progress should remain attached to Part 1.
- Open the Series and choose **Create Part 2**. It should use the same quest, start privately, and let you choose a fresh outing. Finish its video and choose **Publish Part 2** when ready. A repeated quest within the family cooldown should clearly say it earns no second award.
- Leave a Series draft and return through Discover → Series or Profile → Series. Try title changes and part ordering; parts already accepted or filmed must retain their original quest and video.
- Check Discover's aligned filters, top-right search and prominent Series section, then Profile's Series and Private tabs.
- Try the optional **Draft with AI** flow from Create, or AI filming help on a quest. AI runs only after the in-app consent step and can take around a minute. Generated original quest drafts still require review before becoming runnable recommendations.
- Exercise the full-screen camera, hold/release and tap recording, multiple takes, draft restoration, timer, front/rear camera, teleprompter, gallery import and photo overlay. Save and finish the video, download through the iPhone share sheet, and check the Sidequest watermark.
- Try native nearby place search with location allowed and denied. Manual area entry should remain available.

Please report the screen and action where anything fails, the iPhone model and iOS version, and whether it repeats after reopening the app. Real ChatGPT app handoff, camera/microphone interruptions, flash support, email sign-in deep links and saving to Photos need physical-device attention. Confirmed server saves must survive reopening; pending session drafts are not guaranteed after app termination.

Recording supports up to 60 seconds. Optional AI uses OpenAI on the backend; normal recommendations still use reviewed authored activities. Live event-provider inventory is not configured. Event browsing links do not verify tickets, opening hours, venue permission or availability. Sign in with Apple is not part of this beta; the existing email sign-in remains in place.
