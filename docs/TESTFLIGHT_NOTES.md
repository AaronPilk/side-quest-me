# Internal iPhone beta — 1.0.0 (1)

This beta connects to the live Sidequest account, quest and video services. Your videos stay private until you explicitly publish them.

Please try:

- Create a Date Night quest with Full Send, Couple, a $100 total budget, three hours and Outside. The recommendations should preserve those choices. Try the budget slider and exact-amount option.
- Allow location, choose a nearby real place, then try again with location denied and a manually entered area.
- Open the full-screen camera. Try 15/30/60-second limits, hold/release recording, several takes, a countdown, front/rear camera and the editable teleprompter. Flash availability depends on the camera.
- Swipe up for the quest instructions. Import a finished video or add a photo overlay from the gallery. Leave a recording draft and return to it.
- Save and finish a video, download it through the iPhone share sheet and check the Sidequest logo plus “Side quest app” watermark. Verify the image overlay appears where expected.
- Visit Discover, Activity, Create, Rewards and Profile. Edit your profile, try the preference review, and check your personal quest milestones after completing a quest.

Please report the screen and action where anything fails, the iPhone model and iOS version, and whether the problem repeats after reopening the app. Camera/microphone interruptions, real-device flash support, email deep links and saving to Photos need particular attention on physical phones.

Recording currently supports up to 60 seconds. AI quest generation and live event-provider inventory are not configured in this beta; recommendations come from authored activities. Event browsing links do not imply that tickets, opening hours, venue permission or availability are verified.
