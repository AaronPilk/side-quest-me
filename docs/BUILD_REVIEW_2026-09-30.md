## Findings and disposition

### Confirmed, corrected in the later working tree

**Discovery search could overwrite newly typed input.** The baseline reconciled URL query state through a deferred effect. A second query entered immediately after submission could be replaced by the previous query when that effect ran. The baseline browser suite reproduced a search failure. `src/pages/Discover.tsx:495` now reconciles navigation in a layout effect before the next paint/input event. The affected discovery scenarios passed in the follow-up run.

**The reel viewer's title linked to its own detail route.** Clicking that title could replace the original feed return destination with the reel's own route. The detail title is now plain text while feed-card titles remain links (`src/pages/Discover.tsx:140`). The regression check clicks the title before closing and verifies return to the same search/filter (`tests/discovery-audit-browser.spec.ts:76`).

### Confirmed in Claude baseline; corrected in the later content pass

**P2 — Budget filtering uses the lower cost estimate.** `shared/quest-ideas.ts:2624–2627` compares `idea.cost.minMinor` with the user's ceiling. Existing recommendation eligibility compares the maximum estimate (`shared/recommend.ts:130`). Reproduction: `filterQuestIdeas({ budgetMinor: 1000 }, [market_dinner])` admits an idea estimated at 1,000–4,000 cents. A zero budget admits 25 ideas with positive maximum estimates. Use the upper estimate or an explicitly authored free version; the editor should not receive an over-budget draft advertised as fitting a hard ceiling.

**P2 — Generated recipes lose time and participant requirements.** `generateQuestRecipe` (`shared/quest-ideas.ts:2753–2765`) omits the idea's `minutes` and `groups`. `draftVariants` (`shared/quest-ideas.ts:2787–2789`) then uses `recipeVariants`, whose defaults are 25/40/55 minutes and 1–8 participants outside Date Night (`shared/activity-recipes.ts:1171–1173`). Reproduction: the friends-only, 75-minute `reading_party` idea explicitly requires 40 minutes reading plus 10 minutes discussion; its generated Chill variant says 25 minutes and permits one participant. Its review notes do not flag that mismatch. Preserve the requirements through expansion, or require a deliberate editor resolution before producing publishable variants. Schema validity alone does not validate these semantics.

Both baseline defects are now corrected in the working tree: maximum cost is the budget ceiling check, and drafts preserve time, exact allowed groups, cost, intensity and authored briefs. Focused regression tests cover the market dinner and reading party examples. These two issues concerned **editorial drafts**, not current live recommendations. The baseline disclosed cost loss in review notes but did not disclose time/group loss. The 116 ideas are not automatically published into the app's recommendation catalog. Do not “fix” this by editing historical published rows or applied migrations.
